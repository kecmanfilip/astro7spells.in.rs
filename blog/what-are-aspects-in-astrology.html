What Are Aspects in Astrology?
5 Major Aspects Explained

Updated: May 13, 2026 | Astro7Spells Team

If you have ever wondered why some planets in your natal chart seem to "work together" while others create tension, the answer lies in aspects. Aspects are the hidden grammar of astrology — they reveal how different parts of your personality communicate, cooperate, or clash.

1. What Are Aspects in Astrology?

In astrology, an aspect is the angular distance between two planets (or points) in a natal chart. Think of the zodiac as a 360° circle. When two planets are separated by a specific number of degrees, they form an aspect — a relationship that describes how their energies interact.

Aspects are not random. They follow precise geometric angles that have been studied for thousands of years. A skilled astrologer reads these angles the way a musician reads notes — each one carries a distinct quality, tone, and meaning.

Quick analogy: If planets are actors on a stage, aspects are the script — they define whether two characters are allies, rivals, lovers, or strangers.

2. The 5 Major Aspects in Astrology

There are dozens of aspects in astrology, but five are considered major because they appear most frequently and have the strongest influence. Here is each one explained:

☌ Conjunction (0°)

When two planets are in the same place (or very close), they form a conjunction. Their energies merge and act as a single force. This is the most intense aspect — neither planet operates independently.

Example: Sun conjunct Mercury gives a mind that is inseparable from identity. These people think as they are — their self-expression and intellect are one.

⚹ Sextile (60°)

The sextile is a cooperative, easy aspect. It represents opportunities that require some effort to activate. Unlike the effortless trine, a sextile offers potential that must be consciously engaged.

Example: Venus sextile Mars creates natural chemistry between love and desire. Attraction flows easily, but the person must still choose to act on it.

□ Square (90°)

The square is the aspect of tension, friction, and growth. Two planets are in conflict — they want different things. This creates internal pressure that, while uncomfortable, is one of the most powerful drivers of personal development.

Example: Moon square Saturn creates tension between emotional needs and responsibility. The person may feel that their feelings are "not allowed" — but working through this tension builds extraordinary emotional resilience.

△ Trine (120°)

The trine is the most harmonious aspect. Energy flows effortlessly between the two planets. This sounds ideal, but there is a catch — trines can make things so easy that the person never develops the skill fully.

Example: Jupiter trine Sun gives natural confidence, optimism, and luck. The person expands easily — but they must consciously choose where to direct that expansion, or it may become aimless.

☍ Opposition (180°)

The opposition creates a polarity — two planets face each other from opposite sides of the chart. This aspect often manifests through relationships: the person attracts others who embody the energy they struggle to own themselves.

Example: Mars opposition Venus creates a dynamic tension between assertion and receptivity. The person may oscillate between pursuing and withdrawing in relationships, or attract partners who represent their "missing half."

4. How to Read Aspects in Your Natal Chart

Reading aspects is a three-step process:

  1. Identify the planets involved. What do these planets represent? (Example: Sun = identity, Moon = emotions)
  2. Note the aspect type. Is it harmonious (trine, sextile), tense (square, opposition), or merged (conjunction)?
  3. Consider the orb. The orb is how exact the aspect is. A 1° orb is much stronger than an 8° orb. Most astrologers use an 8° orb for major aspects.

Pro tip: Pay attention to aspects involving the Sun, Moon, Ascendant, and Midheaven. These "personal points" make aspects feel more immediate and impactful in daily life.

5. Why Aspects Matter More Than Signs Alone

Many beginners focus only on their Sun sign, but aspects reveal the dynamic story of a chart. Two people can both have Sun in Leo, but if one has Sun trine Jupiter and the other has Sun square Saturn, their experiences of confidence, expansion, and self-expression will be radically different.

Aspects are what make your natal chart yours. They explain why you feel conflicted in certain areas, why some talents come naturally, and why particular themes repeat in your relationships and career.

6. Frequently Asked Questions About Aspects

What are aspects in astrology?

Aspects are the angular relationships between planets in a natal chart. They show how planetary energies interact — whether they cooperate (trine, sextile), clash (square, opposition), or merge (conjunction). The 5 major aspects are conjunction (0°), sextile (60°), square (90°), trine (120°), and opposition (180°).

What is the most powerful aspect?

The conjunction (0°) is generally considered the most powerful because it merges two planetary energies into a single focus. However, squares (90°) and oppositions (180°) can be equally impactful because the tension they create drives action and growth.

Can aspects change in a natal chart?

No — natal chart aspects are fixed because they depend on your exact birth moment. However, transiting planets form temporary aspects to your natal planets, creating shifting influences throughout your life.

What is an "orb" in astrology?

The orb is the allowable margin of error for an aspect. For example, a square is exactly 90°, but planets separated by 85°–95° may still be considered "in square" depending on the orb used. Most astrologers use an 8° orb for major aspects involving the Sun or Moon, and 6° for other planets.
